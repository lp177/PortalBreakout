How to play

  • Paddles are portals. The ball never bounces off them — it teleports out of the other paddle, keeping its direction. Falling balls re-enter from the top; rising balls re-enter from the bottom.
  • Guard both edges. Miss a paddle and the ball is gone. You steer by where the ball enters your paddle — edge hits curve the exit.
  • Solo: arrows move the bottom paddle, A/D the top one (rebind in Options). Mouse drives bottom; on touch, each half of the screen drags its own paddle.
  • Space launches the ball, Esc pauses.
  • Break every brick to clear a level. Silver takes two hits, gold is indestructible, orange cores explode.
  • Catch the drops with either paddle: multiball, lasers, fireball, sticky, expand, slow, extra life.
